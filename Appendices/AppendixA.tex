\newcommand{\gx}{\textcolor{gray!60}{\xmark}}

This appendix contains the complete ablation tables referenced in Chapter~5. Table~\ref{tab:ablation_combinations_full} lists all 32 ablation configurations (the \textit{original} PlanarGS reference row is excluded from ablation tables since it runs different code); Table~\ref{tab:ablation_results} reports quantitative results averaged across three Deep Blending scenes (DrJohnson, Library, Playroom). Tables~A.3--A.5 provide the raw per-scene measurements for all 32 configurations.
